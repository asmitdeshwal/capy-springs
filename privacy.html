Capy Springs – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Applies to Capy Springs on Android, iOS and the web

The short version: Capy Springs has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. The Android and iOS apps show ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information about your device to show those ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, ads are only personalised after you agree, and players who tell the app they are under 13 only ever see non-personalised ads for general audiences. The web version shows no ads.

Who we are

Capy Springs is an independent game made by Asmit Deshwal. Questions: open an issue at github.com/asmitdeshwal/capy-springs/issues.

What the game keeps on your device

Your progress (lanterns, upgrades, koban, Guestbook goals and stamps), your settings (sound, vibration, effects, reminders) and, in the apps, the birth year you gave are saved only on your device. We do not run any server and we never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it; Reset save in Settings deletes your progress.

Ads in the Android and iOS apps (Google AdMob)

The apps show ads provided by Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for an in-game bonus, and an occasional short ad between moments of play. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (city or region, not your exact location);
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped);
  • diagnostic information about how the ads performed.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, keep advertising safe from fraud and abuse, and, only where you have agreed or the law allows it, to personalise ads. Google's own policies describe this in detail: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal information from Google; we only see totals such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, the IDFA is not used. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Videos are optional. No reward in the game requires watching an ad, and nothing in the game can be bought.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules at most two notifications on your own device (for example, when your inn's koban are waiting). They are created and stored on your device only; nothing is sent to us. Turn them off in Settings › Reminders or in your phone's notification settings.

Children and the age question

Capy Springs is made for players of all ages. The first time the Android or iOS app starts, it asks what year you were born. The answer stays on your device; it is never sent to us or to Google. The app uses it only to set up ads safely:

  • Under 13: every ad request is marked as directed to a child, so Google shows only ads suitable for general audiences, does not personalise them and limits the data it uses, as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requires. The app does not ask for consent to personalised ads or for permission to track.
  • Under 16: ad requests are also marked as coming from someone under the age of consent, so ads are not personalised in Europe, and the app does not ask for permission to track.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, and we hold no information about any player. If you are a parent and have a question, contact us at the address above.

US state privacy laws

We do not sell personal information for money. Some US state laws treat the use of advertising identifiers for personalised ads as "sharing". You can opt out at any time by limiting ad tracking on your device as described under Your choices.
